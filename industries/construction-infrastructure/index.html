Operating Reality

Site tooling has a different economics to plant tooling: tools live outdoors, get handled by rotating labour, and go missing. Every purchase decision here is really a decision about how much durability is worth against an accepted loss rate, and pretending otherwise leads to buying premium tools that walk off site in a month.

So the useful advice is not "buy the best". It is knowing where durability genuinely pays (rotary hammers, lifting gear, levels, measuring) and where a consumable-grade tool is the right answer because it will be lost long before it wears out. We will tell you which is which rather than upselling both.

The third thing that matters is delivery. Site stores are not registered offices, and a consignment that arrives where nobody can sign for it is a consignment that has not arrived. The customer base runs from metro and road package contractors and industrial builders around Pithampur and Sanwer through precast yards and RMC plants to MEP, plumbing and interior contractors.

Lifting & Rigging Checklist

Sizing guidance for the equipment a safety audit will actually look at. Everything below gets selected above the maximum load with margin, never at the rated limit, because the rated limit is a test figure, not a working one.

EquipmentRangeSizing note
Hydraulic Bottle Jack2–50 TCheck both capacity and lift height; the second one is what catches people out on a precast job
Table Lifter150 kg upwardFor wheeled and staged loads, not for suspended lifting
Lifting Accessory500–1500 kg stackers, strapsRated and marked; check the marking survives site conditions, not just the first inspection
Hand Truck / TrolleyPlatform and sack trucksThe cheapest way to stop manual handling injuries on a finishing floor
Wire Rope CutterMatched to rope diameterA hacksawed wire rope end is a frayed rope end
Pry Bar · Sledge HammerPositioning and persuasionNeeded alongside every lift, and always forgotten in the quote
Chain pulley block1, 2, 3, 5, 10 TSelect above the maximum load with margin, never at the rated limit; sourced to order, marked and certified
Lever hoist (pull lift)0.75–6 TFor pulling and positioning, not sustained suspension
D-shackles, eye bolts, slingsRated and markedNever substitute unmarked hardware from the local market; this is the one item where the saving is not a saving

Published plainly, because it should be: capacity marking, periodic inspection, and never exceeding rated load. Unmarked lifting hardware bought locally is a genuine risk: it has no traceable proof load, no material certificate and no way for an inspector to accept it, and it fails without warning rather than with it. We would rather lose the order than supply it, and we would rather tell you that than offer a discount.
