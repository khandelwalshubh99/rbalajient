Plastics, Packaging & Printing

Mould change kits, tool room precision and blade continuity for moulders, converters and printers.

Operating Reality

Mould changes are timed and repeated, which makes them the one maintenance activity in this segment that is measured like production. A changeover that takes ninety minutes instead of fifty, three times a week, is a shift a month, and almost all of the difference is tooling and staging, not skill.

Moulds are heavy, precise and expensive, and mishandling costs lakhs rather than thousands. A steel hammer used once for alignment can mark a mould face permanently. That is why the kit below specifies copper and nylon and nothing else, and why lifting hardware has to be rated, marked and thread-matched.

Tool room work demands genuine precision measurement, and converting demands blade continuity: slitting, guillotine and utility blades are a continuous spend whose consumption varies with film gauge and filler content rather than with a calendar. Both of those are planning problems more than purchasing problems, and both are solvable.

Calibration guidance worth publishing: set an interval on anything that produces a recorded number: twelve months is a reasonable default for a caliper or micrometer in daily use. But interval is not the whole rule: a caliper dropped once should be re-verified before it is trusted again, regardless of when it was last calibrated. Damage does not wait for a schedule.

Common Failure Points

Symptom, root cause, and the fix: the conversations we have most often on this shop floor.

Symptom

Mould faces damaged during a change

Cause

Steel hammers used for alignment

Fix

Copper and nylon mallets only, kept on the trolley so the steel one is never the nearest tool

Copper Hammer · Mallet · Brass Hammer
Symptom

Clamp bolts loosening in production

Cause

Torqued by feel, with no documented value

Fix

Torque wrench against a documented value per press, checked at the start of each run

Torque Wrench · T-Handle Hex Key
Symptom

Flash appearing progressively through a run

Cause

Parting line and clamp condition never checked between changes

Fix

Feeler gauge routine at changeover and a clamp inspection on the same sheet

Feeler Gauge · Scraper
Symptom

Guide pin galling

Cause

Wrong grease grade, or the right grease never reaching the pin at temperature

Fix

High-temp grease with a flexible-hose gun that can actually reach the point

Grease Gun · Hose & Nozzle · Grease Cartridge
Symptom

Slit edge quality degrading through a shift

Cause

Blade run past its life because the change interval is a guess

Fix

Blade change interval set from tracked data per machine and film type
