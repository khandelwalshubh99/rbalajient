Operating Reality

A mill is hundreds of identical machines holding thousands of identical small parts. Maintenance here is high-frequency and low-value per event, but the volume is enormous, and that inverts the usual buying logic. Precision small tools matter more than big tools, and replenishment matters more than specification.

The tools a fitter carries are consumables, not assets. Snips blunt, long-nose tips spread, feeler gauges get kinked, precision screwdrivers round off. Budgeting one purchase per fitter and then wondering why settings drift is the most common mistake we see, and it is an expensive one: a worn feeler gauge costs more in machine settings than a year of replacements.

Lubrication schedules are relentless, and they are where the volume sits: oil cans, grease guns, couplers and the right grades, matched to an oiling round that runs whether or not the store has stock. When a mill needs two hundred identical screwdriver sets or five hundred oil cans, that is a stock and pricing problem. It should be ours, not yours.

Common Failure Points

Symptom, root cause, and the fix: the conversations we have most often on this shop floor.

Symptom

Repeated spindle bearing failure on the same frames

Cause

Over-greasing, or the wrong grade applied on a fixed schedule regardless of condition

Fix

Correct grade plus a metered gun so the quantity is controlled rather than judged

Grease Gun · Grease Cartridge · Bearing Puller
Symptom

Machine settings drifting after every service

Cause

Feeler gauges worn, kinked or from an incomplete set

Fix

Fresh feeler gauge sets on a replacement cycle, treating them as consumables so the drift stops

Feeler Gauge
Symptom

Fitters improvising with the wrong tool

Cause

Personal kits not standardised, or issued once and never replenished

Fix

Standard issue kit per fitter with a scheduled replacement, not a requisition process

Screwdriver Set · Tool Bag · Precision Plier
Symptom

Grease gun leaking at every point in the plant

Cause

Coupler and nipple types mismatched across machines, so no single coupler seats properly

Fix

Standardise the nipple type plant-wide, then match the coupler to it
